AUDIO DIRECTION FOR: The Everything Button

===========================================================
1. THREE AUDIO DIRECTION CONCEPTS
===========================================================

--- Concept A: "Carnival Factory" (toy-machine carnival-electro) ---
Style:      Upbeat carnival-electro / 8-bit big-top hybrid. Bouncy square/saw
            leads over a driving four-on-the-floor kick, with calliope-like
            arpeggios and gear-ratchet percussion. A continuous low machine hum
            (sine + detuned saw) runs underneath everything and rises in pitch
            during button charge.
Instrumentation (all synthesized): square lead, detuned saw pads, triangle
            bass, noise-based snare/hat, filtered noise "gear ticks", calliope
            arpeggio, low sine hum.
Mood:       Cheerful, chaotic, slightly unhinged — a prize machine that is
            having too much fun dispensing the universe. Bright and inviting
            for beginners, escalating to manic by Zone 5.
Fit:        Directly matches §5's "carnival-bright toy-machine aesthetic" and
            §6's "upbeat carnival-electro bed." The charge-tied hum satisfies
            the GDD's explicit requirement that the button's hum track charge.
            Tempo/layer step per zone gives the required linear intensity ramp.

--- Concept B: "Overclocked Arcade" (synthwave / arcade chiptune) ---
Style:      Retro synthwave arcade pulse. Pulsing saw bassline, gated pad
            chords, chiptune leads, hard electronic drums. Cold neon feel.
Instrumentation: saw bass, gated pads, pulse-wave lead, noise drums, ping SFX.
Mood:       Sleek, fast, confident, slightly sinister. High-energy but cool
            rather than warm.
Fit:        Matches "Overclocked" zone naming and the arcade hybrid genre, but
            its cool neon mood conflicts with the GDD's explicitly warm
            carnival palette (#FF4757/#FFA502) and "toy-machine" shape language.
            Also undersells the comedy/meta-humor of the "ANYTHING GOES" gag.

--- Concept C: "Workshop Idle" (lo-fi chill / cozy incremental) ---
Style:      Mellow lo-fi loop with soft Rhodes, brushed percussion, and warm
            sub-bass. Relaxed tempo with gentle jazz extensions.
Instrumentation: Rhodes electric piano, upright sub-bass, brushed noise hats,
            soft pad, occasional bell.
Mood:       Calm, cozy, ambient, non-intrusive. Good for long idle sessions.
Fit:        Suits incremental players' long-tail sessions and the meta-progression
            layer, but it is tonally wrong for an arcade tap-hold-drag game with
            1.8 s token lifetimes and jackpot confetti in Zone 5. No room for the
            charge-tied drama the GDD requires. Risk of the game feeling lifeless.

===========================================================
2. SELECTION NOTE (transparent reasoning)
===========================================================
Selection: CONCEPT A — "Carnival Factory".

Scoring (1-5 each, weighted to the GDD's stated priorities):

  Criterion (weight)               A     B     C
  ------------------------------------------------
  Theme fit — "Anything Goes" (25%) 5     4     3
  GDD Audio Spec match (§6)   (25%) 5     4     2
  Visual palette coherence (§5)(20%) 5    3     3
  Genre/audience fit          (20%) 4     4     3
  Escalation headroom (zones) (10%) 5     4     2
  ------------------------------------------------
  Weighted total                    4.85  3.85  2.70

Why A wins:
- It is the only concept that satisfies every explicit sentence in GDD §6:
  "looping upbeat carnival-electro bed," a per-zone added layer/tempo notch, and
  a button hum "tied to the charge state." B and C each miss at least one.
- It reconciles the game's two tonal halves — beginner-friendly warmth (Zone 1)
  and manic challenge (Zone 5) — through a single linearly escalating arrangement,
  which is exactly the GDD's "learn → challenge" curve expressed in sound.
- Carnival timbres (calliope, ratchets, machine hum) sonically mirror the
  chunky chrome-and-gears toy machine described in §5, so audio and visuals sell
  the same fiction instead of competing.

Residual from B/C folded into A:
- B's arcade pulse supplies Zone 4-5's "Overclocked" energy (tighter drums,
  faster arp) rather than a separate style.
- C's warmth is kept as A's Zone 1 palette (softer lead, slower tempo, sparse
  percussion) so the game never feels harsh to a first-timer.

===========================================================
MUSIC
===========================================================
A looping, upbeat CARNIVAL-FACTORY ELECTRO bed — think a joyous prize machine
staffed by slightly unhinged robots. Foundation is a 4/4 electro pulse with a
triangle/sine bass and noise-based kick/snare/hat. Over it sits a bright square
lead trading phrases with a calliope-style arpeggio (fast ascending/descending
runs), colored by detuned saw pads. A constant low "machine hum" (sine + gently
detuned saw, ~55 Hz) underlies the mix; when the player holds the ANYTHING GOES
button, that hum rises in pitch and adds a gain wobble so the machine audibly
"charges up." Gear-ratchet ticks (bandpass-filtered noise bursts) faintly mark
beats, reinforcing the mechanical fiction.

Intensity is LINEAR across the five zones, one notch per zone:
  Zone 1 "The Warm-Up"  — 104 BPM, bass + soft lead only, sparse percussion,
                          warm and forgiving.
  Zone 2 "Getting Weird"— 112 BPM, add hat + calliope arpeggio.
  Zone 3 "Anything Goes"— 120 BPM, add pads + ratchet ticks, minor color shift.
  Zone 4 "Overclocked"  — 128 BPM, add off-beat bass stabs + snare fills.
  Zone 5 "The Everything"—136 BPM, full arrangement, busier arpeggio, brighter
                          lead; a fifth layer of high sparkle accents.
  Endless mode continues the same +8 BPM-per-zone step, capped at 160 BPM.

Zone transitions do not hard-cut: the current loop bar finishes, the new tempo
and layer fade in over ~1.5 s under the zone-transition fanfare (fanfare ducks
music per §6 mix rules). Jackpots briefly duck the music ~−6 dB and layer a
triumphant major jingle. On stability < 30, the music bed high-passes and a
heartbeat pulse (two soft thumps) emerges; on Game Over the bed stops for the
sad-trombone stinger.

===========================================================
SOUND EFFECTS
===========================================================
Every SFX is a short synthesized envelope (oscillator + noise + filter) defined
per event. "Bus" = sfx bus unless noted.

- button_press (thunk + charge whir): On pointer down inside the button core.
  Soft mechanical thunk (low sine thump, 90 ms) + a rising charge whir whose
  pitch/gain tracks hold duration continuously (loopable saw w/ lowpass sweep).
- roll_release (slot clatter + gear ticks): On release of the button. Burst of
  bandpass-filtered noise clatter (slot reels) and 3-5 gear-ratchet ticks,
  pitch-bending down as the outcome token pops out.
- token_spawn (springy pop): On token appearance. Short pitch-up blip (15 ms
  triangle) + tiny noise transient; reinforces the squash-and-stretch spawn.
- resource_collect (coin chime): Correct Resource routed to VAULT. Bright
  two-note coin chime (sine/bell FM); STARTING PITCH RISES ONE SEMITONE PER
  COMBO STEP so combo build is audibly rewarding (x1 C5 → x8 C6).
- upgrade_install (socket clack + shimmer): Correct Upgrade routed to SOCKET.
  Snappy wooden clack (filtered square click) followed by a sci-fi shimmer
  (ascending high FM/wavetable sweep, ~300 ms).
- enemy_defeat (cartoon splat/pop): Correct Enemy routed to CANNON. Squashed
  noise "splat" (fast pitch-down) + a bright pop; adds a +10 coin tick when the
  Hot Cannon module is active.
- wrong_route (gentle sad-trombone blip): Wrong zone or expired token. Soft,
  never harsh descending "wah-wah" blip (filtered saw, 250 ms, low volume);
  intentionally comedic to match "never harsh" in GDD §6.
- near_miss (klaxon jingle + applause): Routing within the final 0.4 s window
  (or enemy near expiry, or junk edge-flick in final 0.3 s). Distinct two-tone
  klaxon (high, urgent but friendly) layered over a light synthesized applause
  burst; fires with the "NEAR MISS +50" popup.
- zone_transition (rising arpeggio fanfare): On entering each new zone. Fast
  ascending major arpeggio fanfare; transposed ONE SEMITONE HIGHER PER ZONE
  (Zone 1 root → Zone 5 root+4), with a brief duck of the music bus.
- combo_milestone (chain chime): On combo reaching x5 and x8. Bright ascending
  bell cluster; plays over the resource chime.
- jackpot (triumphant jingle + confetti sparkle): Perfect T3 hold + correct
  route in Zone 5 (or later zones in endless). Music ducks ~−6 dB; a multi-note
  triumphant brass-ish jingle (stacked saws + octave lead) plus a shimmering
  high sparkle and one full-screen power chord. Pairs with the confetti burst.
- stability_warning (low warning thrum): On any Stability loss. Low pulsing
  thrum (sub sine + noise, ~180 ms); pitch and urgency scale with the size of
  the loss. Below 30 Stability, a recurring heartbeat pulse (two soft thumps)
  plays on a slow loop until recovery or death.
- game_over (sad-trombone stinger): Stability hits 0. Descending pitch-bend
  trombone figure (filtered saw with vibrato) ~1.2 s; music bed stops first.
- victory (explosive fanfare): Zone 5 meter completes. Extended major fanfare
  (stacked saws, bell accents, rising run) ~2.5 s; confetti-sparkle tail.
- ui_click (soft tick): Menu button presses (Title/Pause/Settings/How-To).
  Short, quiet sine tick — subtle, no fatigue across repeated taps.
- ui_toggle (switch snap): Music/SFX/Reduced-flash toggles. Crisp two-part
  click; when turning MUSIC on, a short level-confirm chord plays if enabled.
- title_demo_roll (harmless mini-roll): Tapping the decorative Title button.
  A condensed, quieter roll_release + a single coin plink — the "delightful
  micro-interaction" from GDD §8 Screen 1.
- pause_in / resume_out (soft whoosh): Entering/leaving Pause. Quick filtered
  noise whoosh; pause additionally pitch-downs the music bed and freezes it
  (per §7), SFX already in flight allowed to finish.

SFX MIX RULES
- SFX bus ducks ~−4 dB for the duration of zone_transition and jackpot; music
  bus ducks ~−6 dB for jackpot.
- Combo-pitched resource chimes keep their own headroom so the pitch ladder
  stays legible over music.
- All warning/klaxon SFX capped below music peaks so they inform without alarm.

===========================================================
TECHNICAL APPROACH
===========================================================
- Single self-contained index.html; NO external audio assets. All sound is
  synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API (OscillatorNode, GainNode,
  BiquadFilterNode, WaveShaperNode, BufferSourceNode for noise). This satisfies
  AGENTS.md's no-download single-file requirement and GDD §10.3.
- One AudioContext created (and resumed) on the first user gesture to satisfy
  browser autoplay policy; every SFX function no-ops gracefully if the context
  is missing/suspended, so the game never throws.
- Node graph (GDD §10.3): source(s) -> busGain(music | sfx) -> masterGain ->
  destination. Music scheduler uses a lookahead queue (setInterval ~25 ms
  scheduling ~100 ms ahead) driven by performance.now() to avoid timer drift;
  tempo/layer changes apply on bar boundaries for click-free transitions.
- Noise buffers (white/pink) are generated once and reused; short noise bursts
  are the backbone of percussion, clicks, ratchets, splats, and whooshes.
- SFX envelope helper: playTone({type, freq, dur, attack, decay, filter, gain,
  pitchBend, detune, pan}) and playNoise({dur, filterType, freq, Q, gain,
  pitchBend}) cover every event above. All nodes are scheduled and auto-stopped
  (osc.stop(t+dur)) and disconnected on `onended` to prevent node leaks in the
  long-running idle loop.
- Voice/polyphony cap: limit simultaneous SFX voices (e.g., 24) and steal the
  oldest; pooled noise sources. Combo chimes retrigger without stacking
  indefinitely.
- Toggles: Pause/Settings flip `musicBus.gain.value` / `sfxBus.gain.value`
  between 0 and their base level (with a 100 ms ramp to avoid clicks) and
  persist to localStorage key `teb_settings { music, sfx, reducedFlash }`.
  Music scheduler keeps running silently when muted so state stays consistent.
- Pause/visibility: on pause or `visibilitychange` (auto-pause per §7), ramp the
  music bus to 0 and suspend scheduling; on resume, re-queue from the next bar.
  In-flight one-shot SFX are allowed to finish (short envelopes, no residue).
- Performance: precompute noise buffers and reuse; avoid per-frame allocation in
  the SFX path; total audio cost is negligible next to canvas particles. Audio
  degrades gracefully — if AudioContext is unavailable, the game runs silent.
- Accessibility: audio is informational, never required to play; music and SFX
  are independently mutable; the heartbeat/warning cues also have visual
  counterparts (Stability bar flash), so no gameplay meaning depends on audio.
