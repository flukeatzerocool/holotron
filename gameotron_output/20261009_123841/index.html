HOW TO PLAY

◉
1. TAP & HOLD the big ANYTHING GOES button to charge the roll. Hold longer for a better tier.
🎲
2. RELEASE — the machine dispenses a random outcome token. A per-zone lifetime clock starts ticking.
➜
3. DRAG the token to its matching zone: Resource → VAULT (right), Upgrade → SOCKET (top), Enemy → CANNON (left).
🗑
4. FLICK JUNK off any screen edge to clear it (appears Zone 3+).
★
5. CHAIN correct routes for combo points. Route in the final 0.4s for a NEAR MISS +50.
♥
6. PROTECT STABILITY. An enemy that expires costs 12 Stability. Reach 0 and the machine dies.

Touch: tap-hold-slide-release.   Mouse: click-hold-drag-release. Same gestures on phone or PC.
