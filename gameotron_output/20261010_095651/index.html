How to Play

The candle is your clock, your health, and your map. Every turn burns 1 wax. As wax falls your light radius closes in. At 0 wax the guttering dark begins.

Move: tap/click an adjacent room tile, or use WASD / arrow keys. One move = one wax. Space waits (burns 1 wax).

Creatures: choose Run (spills wax) or Endure (spills wax and snuffs the candle — you need a match to re-light). There is no HP and no battle.

Matches re-light a snuffed candle (restores it to 8 wax). The key opens the ground-floor exit.

Survivors (floor 2 down): six actions. Warm acts (share light, ally, bargain, trade) lower Dread but cost light. Cold acts (steal, threaten) buy light now but raise Dread.

Dread (0–8) is weather: it starts strangers warier and speeds Orlok when your flame dies. Warm acts make it roll away.

Descend: stand in a Descending Stair and press Descend (or move down). Reach the ground floor with the key to escape.

Lose: the only death is Count Orlok after the grace turns run out while your candle is out.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move · Space wait/confirm · Esc pause · 1–6 social actions.
