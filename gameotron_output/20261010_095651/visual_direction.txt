Rationale: it is the only concept where the signature ("the light is the

mask is the frame; silhouette carries all meaning; the ≤3-hue rule and the
375 px floor are naturally satisfied because nothing depends on fine detail.
It is also the cheapest to implement canvas-only (one radial gradient, a

(void breathing pulse + spill ring + flicker), per spec.

======================================================================
1. ART STYLE
======================================================================
**Void-Carve.** Canvas-2D, flat shapes lit only by a single radial candle
mask. The world has no ambient fill: any pixel outside the mask is `#000`.
Inside the mask, geometry is drawn as **solid silhouettes** (no outlines, no
gradients except the candle's own falloff) and coloured by a per-archetype
stone/wood/iron tint. The mask's soft edge + spill ring are the *only*
lighting model. Reads as German-Expressionist woodcut / 1922 Nosferatu:
stark warped angles, long clawed outlines, low-key. **Silhouette first** —
if a shape is not identifiable as a black cut-out at the radius edge, it is
drawn wrong.

No anti-aliased detail; no textures; no external images or fonts. All glyphs
drawn from primitives (`arc`, `rect`, `path`, `triangle`, `quadraticCurveTo`).

======================================================================
2. COLOR PALETTE
======================================================================
Three hue families only. Absolute black is a *value*, not a hue — it never
tints. Reserved red is a hue but is **never** used for anything but danger.

- Primary (void / ink):        #000000   — absolute black. HARD LOCK: never
  lightened above #050403 (see "void discipline" below).
- Secondary (flame core):      #FFD27A   amber-gold core, hottest point
- Secondary (flame mid):       #E8912B   amber body of the flame / lit-object key
- Secondary (flame deep):      #8A3D12   ember base, candle-stand glow, mask mid
- Tertiary (cold stone):       #4C5A6B   desaturated grey-blue at the radius edge
- Tertiary (cold stone dim):   #2A333D   deep stone in the spill ring
- Reserved (danger/Orlok):     #C01A1A   arterial red — Orlok, blood, damage,
                                          near-miss flash edge. NOTHING else.

Derived (no new hues, alpha/luma only): warm falloff = radial gradient
#FFD27A → #E8912B → #8A3D12 → transparent; spill = dim grey-blue alpha
0.10–0.16; void breathing tint shift uses alpha on #000 only.

**Void discipline (rule):** black stays `#000000`–`#050403`. It must never
drift toward grey. The known/unknown border is the one line that must always
be readable, so the *cold* hues live only inside/beyond the spill ring and
never in the void proper. Test: screenshot a scoping-room frame — if the
black reads as charcoal rather than void, fail.

**Three-hue audit:** on any gameplay frame, count hues = amber/gold (1),
grey-blue (1), red (1, only if danger present). That is the ceiling.

======================================================================
3. MOOD / ATMOSPHERE
======================================================================
Claustrophobic, patient, candle-close. Empty black is the antagonist; the
flame is a character you are always about to lose. Light is never "on" — it
*leans*, *breathes*, and *closes in*. The mood must be calm enough that a
hard snuff (instant black) hits like a slammed door and a snuff reads
differently from the slow guttering dark. Dread is weather: the colour of the
dark shifts, not the geometry.

======================================================================
4. KEY VISUAL ELEMENTS (canvas-drawn)
======================================================================
- **Candle mask** — radial gradient light on the player; the signature. (5.)
- **Flame sprite (as a character)** — irregular low-amplitude flicker at
  rest (2–4 px lateral wander, 8–14 Hz with a slow 1.2 s beat); leans toward
  the direction of last movement and toward the room's lit neighbour;
  **guttering** = slow 0.6 Hz wobble, tall thin taper, radius trailing to 1;
  **snuff** = frame-hard cut to full black, then a single match-strike
  ember drawn one frame (~120 ms) at the player position.
- **Player survivor** — long-coat silhouette, small warm rim only.
- **Five NPC silhouettes** — distinct outlines readable at the edge:
  Vlask (hunched, boxy, holds a lantern-cage = hoard), Sister Marta (tall,
  rectangular veil/book), Ilona (curved, hunched, one shoulder dropped =
  bitten), Petr (small, round-headed, sticks close), Nadja (lean, angular,
  satchel, mid-stride).
- **Six creature silhouettes** — must read as pure black cut-outs:
  bats (scalloped wings), rats (low pellet cluster + tail), spiders
  (long segmented legs), wolves (long quadruped, low head), shades
  (tall wavering sheet with a gap where the face should be), light-eater
  (a black void-within-void, inverse silhouette that *eats* the mask edge).
- **Orlok** — the only red subject: tall, bald, long clawed coat, pointed
  ears, one-frame red outline at the mask edge as he closes. Never a normal
  encounter sprite.
- **Gothic set pieces** — arches, iron sconces, caged stairwells (bars
  drawn as thin silhouettes over warm stair interior), warped Expressionist
  cabinet angles; ancestral portraits are black canvases with two warm
  eye-glints that track the player's flame (cheap: two 2 px gold dots whose
  x/y lerp toward the player each second).
- **Archetype tints** (silhouette fill only, all within palette):
  spire-entry = near-black stone; stair-corridor = cold grey-blue
  #2A333D; candle-nook = warm ember #8A3D12; shadow-hall = black + red
  glints; scribe-alcove = cold grey with amber paper glint; survivor-camp
  = warm amber edge #E8912B; descending-stair = deep ember + arc.
- **Atmospheric effects (required)** — (a) **void breathing**: full-screen
  black overlay alpha 0.00↔0.04, 6 s period, brightness swells inward
  (never tints — value pulse only); (b) **spill ring** shimmer; (c) **mask
  edge red pulse** on near-miss / damage; (d) **dust motes**: 8–14 slow gold
  specks inside the mask only; (e) ember sparks on a wax pickup.

======================================================================
5. THE CANDLE MASK / FALLOFF SPEC (signature — implement exactly)
======================================================================
Coordinates: mask is centred on the player's tile-centre, in *tile units*,
converted to device pixels. Radius `R_px = R_tiles × tile_px × tile_px`.

`R_tiles` = GDD radius formula: `clamp(base − penalty(wax), min(base,2), base)`,
base 3→7, **flattened** penalty 0 (wax ≥18) / 1 (6–17) / 2 (1–5): the lit radius never
drops below 2 tiles while the flame burns, and at wax = 0 (or a snuff) → R = 1 (guttering).
Runtime-tunable via `window.TUNING` (A/B). Radius changes are tweened over 220 ms so the
frame visibly *closes in* rather than jumping.

Gradient stops (normalised to R):
  0.00  rgba(255,210,122, 0.98)   core
  0.10  rgba(255,210,122, 0.92)
  0.28  rgba(232,145, 43, 0.80)   body
  0.55  rgba(138, 61, 18, 0.55)   ember falloff
  0.82  rgba( 76, 90,107, 0.22)   cold stone enters
  1.00  rgba( 76, 90,107, 0.00)   soft edge

**Soft edge:** the last 18% of R is a fade band — no hard circle. Objects
must resolve *through* the edge, not pop.

**Spill ring (second, dimmer halo):** centre same; stops at 1.00–1.30×R:
  1.00  rgba(76,90,107, 0.16)
  1.15  rgba(76,90,107, 0.09)
  1.30  rgba(76,90,107, 0.00)
Near-things resolve in the spill before entering the main radius (this is
how a creature is spotted at the edge). Spill is cold-grey-blue only — it is
where the *stone* hue lives.

**Implementation:** render the room to an offscreen canvas (silhouettes at
full opacity), then composite through the mask: draw offscreen, then
`globalCompositeOperation = 'destination-in'` with the radial gradient, then
draw the result over the black background. Draw the spill pass separately
at low alpha. Keep the void as a true `#000` clear each frame.

**Mask-edge danger flash:** on near-miss or damage, stroke the outer edge
(0.92–1.00 R) with rgba(192,26,26,a) where a = 0.5 decaying over 350 ms, plus
1-frame full-radius red alpha 0.12. Red ONLY here + Orlok + blood.

**Weather on the mask (wax + Dread):** the falloff's cold end shifts with
Dread (see §6); low wax tightens the gradient (core band compressed, edge
band widened so the shrinking reads). Guttering: gradient pulses with the
slow wobble; snuff: mask alpha → 0 on one frame.

======================================================================
7. TILE / SPRITE GUIDANCE (canvas primitives only)
======================================================================
- **Tile size:** `tile_px = max(48, floor(min(vw, vh) / 12))` at runtime;
  48 px is the hard floor. At 375×667 → 48 px tiles, ~7 tiles across. The
  mask radius (3–7 tiles) is therefore legible at every viewport.
- **Grid:** rooms drawn as filled quads per tile; walls as 3–4 px dark
  strokes inset from tile edge; no texture. Doors/stairs = single bright
  glyph (arch = 2 arcs + amber fill).
- **Silhouette rule:** every entity is a filled `path` in near-black
  (#050403) with ONE warm rim (1 px #E8912B) on the side facing the flame.
  The rim is what makes it readable inside the mask; without it, entities
  vanish into the stone tint.
- **Animation:** 2-frame idle only (flame more; entities a 1 px bob). No
  sprite sheets, no rotation beyond ±20° Expressionist lean. Everything is
  procedural.
- **Camera:** 1-tile glide (120 ms ease-out) on each move — movement is
  punctuation. The camera and the mask centre glide together so the light
  *travels* with you.
- **Damage/near-miss:** the affected entity flickers red for 2 frames; the
  mask edge flashes red (§5).
- **Performance guard:** silhouette pass to offscreen canvas; mask via one
  `destination-in` composite; cap dust motes at 14; all effects are
  additive overlays, never re-rendered geometry.

======================================================================
9. TECHNICAL NOTES
======================================================================
- Canvas 2D only; responsive to `window.innerWidth/Height` and DPR (cap
  at 2× to protect perf). `requestAnimationFrame` loop for flicker/breathe/
  glide; gameplay logic remains turn-driven (visuals are overlays on state).
- No external assets: no images, no web fonts, no audio samples. All shapes
  from primitives; all text from system fonts (sans/serif fallback stack).
- Mask compositing is the perf hotspot: one offscreen silhouette canvas +
  one `destination-in` gradient pass + one spill pass per frame. Never
  stroke per-tile gradients; batch to the offscreen buffer.
- The mask radius, Dread tier colour, and guttering state are read from the
  same game state the HUD reads — single source of truth, no duplicated
  constants.
- Accessibility/readability guard: at 375 px, verify (a) every creature
  silhouette still reads as a distinct cut-out at the spill edge, (b) all
  tappable targets are ≥ 48 px, (c) black never greys out. If any fails,
  reduce detail — never add it.
- Reduced-motion: if `prefers-reduced-motion`, halve flicker amplitude,
  freeze the void breathe at a.03, keep the 1-tile glide but shorten to
  80 ms. The mask/falloff and red flashes always remain (they carry meaning).
