# Torre Nosferatu — Dread Weather

*Escape a pitch-black, candle-lit tower before Count Orlok takes your light.*

A turn-based, grid-based gothic-horror roguelike **descent** for the Gameotron Game Jam.
Jam theme: **Anything Goes** — turned inward: the tower is a lawless jumble of rooms,
floors, and encounters that obey no single logic. Anything goes *in the dark*.

## The hook

**Your candle is your clock, your health, and your map.**

Every step burns wax. As the wax falls, the circle of light around you shrinks — and
everything outside it is absolute black. At zero wax the flame gutters, and Count Orlok
starts to close. Find wax, matches, and the key to the ground-floor door. Get out on your
own light — or slip through a survivor's borrowed door.

## The descent

- Wake at the **Spire** (top floor) with one candle and work **down** toward the only exit.
- Each floor is **generated at runtime** from a world template, seeded per run — a winding
  graph of stairwells, loops, and dead ends, with some stairs skipping a tier.
- Every floor adds **exactly one new pressure**: social encounters, creature density,
  traps, darkness, then ambient wax drain on the ground floor. A run lasts ~5–8 minutes
  (~75–100 turns).
- **Dread Weather** — a single tower-wide 0–8 tug-of-war dial. Cold acts buy light now and
  raise Dread; warm acts lower it and spend light. Dread sets how wary the survivors are
  and how fast Orlok closes.
- **Avoidance, not attrition** — no HP, no attack rolls. Creatures are decisions: *Run*
  spills wax, *Endure* spills wax and snuffs your candle (you'll need a match).
- **Five recurring survivors** — Vlask, Sister Marta, Ilona, Petr, Nadja — each with a
  disposition, goals, and a voice. Trade, warm, or rob; the tower remembers.
- **Scoring** rewards nerve: Near-Miss Bonuses, per-floor Zone Bonuses, lore, and a full
  keyed escape (1000) vs. the reduced borrowed-door escape (400).

The only death is Orlok by wax-out — and only after the guttering-dark grace is spent.

## Controls

Touch and mouse are both fully supported; keyboard is enabled for this run.

- **Touch:** tap an adjacent tile to move (1 wax); tap UI buttons to act; long-press a tile
  to inspect (free); swipe a side panel to open/close the action tray.
- **Mouse:** click to move/act exactly like tap; hover highlights the reachable tile and
  previews its cost.
- **Keyboard:** WASD / arrow keys move one tile; **Space** waits/confirms; **Esc** opens
  Pause; **1–6** select a social action when the social panel is open.

Seven screens: Title, How to Play, Gameplay, Pause (music/SFX toggles, How to Play,
resume, back), Settings (music/SFX, reset run, credits), Game Over, and Victory.

## Notes

- Single HTML file, vanilla JavaScript, all HTML/CSS/JS embedded — no external assets.
- Procedural Web Audio (oscillators + noise) for music and SFX; togglable in Pause and
  Settings, with reduced-motion support.
- Runs in the browser at desktop, laptop, and phone sizes (verified at 1920x1080, 800x600,
  and 375x667).
