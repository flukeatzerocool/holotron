WINNING CONCEPT: #7 — Rule Breaker Dungeon

Genre: grid puzzle-dungeon crawler.

Core touch mechanic: tap tiles to move; each room displays a rising rule
("no going left," "gravity is up," "walls are edible") that you must break
or exploit.

USP: Rules are meant to be shattered — the win condition is often to violate
the displayed law.

Target audience: puzzle veterans and meta-game fans.

Theme integration: A dungeon whose rulebook is torn, where "anything goes" —
the game dares you to ignore the rules and rewards rebellion.

Game feel: Rules shatter with glass-crack SFX, glitch-static transitions,
screen-tilt when gravity flips, escalating alarm pulses, stark dungeon tiles
with rebellious graffiti.

Expert review (browsergame_expert): "Win by breaking the rule" is a genuinely
inventive inversion and perfectly embodies "anything goes." Grid-tap movement
is touch-simple. Risk: player confusion — onboarding must teach the meta gently.
Great escalation potential (rising rules). Very buildable in vanilla JS with a
tile grid. Sharp concept with real personality; a standout.
