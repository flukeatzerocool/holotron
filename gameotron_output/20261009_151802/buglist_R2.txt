[HIGH] East and West door buttons render entirely outside the stage and are
clipped off-screen, so the door-tap travel verb (GDD §4/§7b) is dead for two
of the four directions on every room and viewport.
  Location: index.html:984-989 (DOOR_DEF), 1002-1003 (syncDoors cx),
            93-98 (CSS .doorBtn), 22-25 (#stage overflow:hidden)
  Explanation: syncDoors computes `cx = g.ox + g.tile*g.cols*def.x`. With
  DOOR_DEF.east.x = 1.38 and DOOR_DEF.west.x = -0.38, and because every room
  produces bw = g.tile*g.cols = 432 while the board margins are only
  gx = 24 (BOARD.gx), the buttons land far outside the 480-wide design
  stage: east cx = 24 + 432*1.38 = 620.16; west cx = 24 - 432*0.38 = -140.16.
  #stage has overflow:hidden, so both are clipped and unclickable. Runtime
  confirmation (Firefox, 1280x720, chalk-corridor, stage left=424 right=856):
    EAST  button rect x 956..1008  (fully right of stage right 856)
    WEST  button rect x 272..324   (fully left of stage left 424)
  Rooms with east/west exits (chalk-corridor, gravity-well, torn-library,
  bell-chamber, rule-core) therefore cannot be left via the advertised
  "tap a lit door" control. Traversal still limp-walks via the D-pad
  off-edge path (movePlayer -> doorAttempt) and the keyboard, which masks
  the failure in casual play, but the core GDD door interaction is broken.
  Fix approach: position side doors relative to the board with a margin
  that fits the board's 24 px side gutters (or draw them as overlays inside
  the board edges). E.g. use explicit pixel offsets: east = g.ox+g.bw+14,
  west = g.ox-14 (and clamp/INSIDE variants), or move side doors to the
  board's left/right edge columns. Verify all four buttons have rects fully
  within the stage at 1920x1080, 800x600 and 375x667.

[MEDIUM] North door button overlaps the RULE banner centre and intercepts
presses meant for the banner long-press (blocks the M4 hold on the exact
spot where the rule text sits).
  Location: index.html:1002-1003 (north cy = g.oy - 0.34*bh), 984-985,
            33-47 (#banner), 1078-1091 (banner long-press), 990-995
            (doorLayer z-index 8 appended last)
  Explanation: DOOR_DEF.north.y = -0.34 places the button 0.34 board-heights
  above the board top. For entry-hall (g.oy=254, bh=432 at 1280x720 the
  design oy=204) the north button sits at design y≈57, i.e. inside the
  0-96 topbar where the banner lives. doorLayer is appended last with
  z-index:8 and pointer-events:auto, so it paints over and receives events
  ahead of the banner (z-index auto). Runtime confirmation (entry-hall):
  banner rect x 429..793, y 4..83; document.elementFromPoint(640,43) returns
  `doorBtn` (the north button) — the topmost element at the banner's centre
  is the door, not the banner. Consequences: the rule text is visually
  covered by a lock icon in entry-hall/torn-library, and long-pressing the
  centre of the banner (the obvious place) triggers the door instead of the
  rule-detail card. Rooms with a north exit: entry-hall, chalk-corridor,
  torn-library.
  Fix approach: place the north button in the board's top gutter (design
  y≈150-24) rather than inside the topbar, or raise #topbar/banner above
  doorLayer for the topband and make the door layer non-overlapping. After
  the E/W fix, re-check elementFromPoint at the banner centre returns
  #banner in every room.

[MEDIUM] Near-miss score popups are offset by half a tile because the
position is centred twice.
  Location: index.html:852-857 (nearMiss), 780-785 (addScore), 833/848
            (call sites in stepHazards)
  Explanation: stepHazards calls nearMiss(pcx,pcy) with already-centred tile
  coordinates (player.c+0.5 / bell.c+0.5). nearMiss forwards them to
  addScore(50,'NEAR MISS +50',cx,cy,...), and addScore does
  `popups.push({x:c+0.5, y:r+0.5, ...})` — adding +0.5 again. The popup is
  therefore drawn at (c+1.0, r+1.0), half a tile down-right of the player
  / bell. Other addScore callers pass integer c/r, so only the near-miss
  bonus is affected. Visual glitch only; gameplay unaffected.
  Fix approach: pass integer tile coords from nearMiss (or add a variant of
  addScore that takes centred coordinates), e.g. nearMiss should push the
  popup at the coords it is given.
