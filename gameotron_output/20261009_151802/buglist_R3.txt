[M1] Side-door DOM buttons now overlay the board's outermost columns and
  intercept tap-to-move for the edge cells, so tapping a tile centre there
  performs the door action (travel or "DOOR LOCKED") instead of moving.
  This is a regression introduced by the R2 door repositioning (R2 placed the
  side doors on the board edges to escape #stage clipping; they now sit on top
  of the edge column at the board's vertical middle).
  Location: index.html:1012-1013 (east/west placement), 93-101
            (.doorBtn 52px fixed box, transform:translate(-50%,-50%)),
            1063-1070 (canvas pointer handlers never fire because the door
            button is the topmost element), 735-748 (handleTap tap-to-move)
  Explanation: syncDoors clamps the side doors to the board edge / stage gutter
  rather than leaving a clear tile. At scale < 1 (mobile/small) hw = 26/scale
  grows in design units, so the button covers more of the edge column.
  Independent runtime verification:
    - 375x667 (scale 0.781): tile centres (0,2)->WEST and (6,2)->EAST are
      covered by doorBtn (elementFromPoint returns the button); clicking the
      centre of (6,2) leaves the player at (5,2) (interior (4,2) still MOVES).
    - 800x600 (scale 0.75): east edge (6,2) centre hit doorBtn -> NOT MOVED;
      west edge (0,2) centre hit doorBtn -> NOT MOVED (travel fired once the
      rule was broken by the interior westward move). Interior taps MOVED.
    - 1920x1080: edge tile centre is just outside the button; only the outer
      fringe of the edge cell is covered.
  Impact: the primary advertised control ("tap the tile you want to step onto",
  How to Play, index.html:205) silently does the wrong thing on the outer
  columns at the door row on the mobile and desktop-small viewports. The D-pad
  / keyboard remain as workarounds, and the door itself is a legitimate tap
  target, so this is rated MEDIUM (borderline HIGH on mobile, where the covered
  area is ~60% of the cell).
  Fix approach: keep the door hit target clear of tile centres — e.g. inset the
  side doors fully into the stage gutter by shrinking the button at small scales
  (size = min(52, gutter*scale) or a smaller min target), offset the button
  centre to the outer ~10px of the stage, or render side doors as narrow
  edge chevrons/half-buttons anchored to the stage border so they never cover a
  tile centre. Re-run the edge-tile elementFromPoint + tap-move probe on all
  three viewports after the change.

[M2] Bell Chamber near-miss bonus repeats indefinitely after the bell is rung,
  granting +50 every ~3s while the player stands on the bell (score farm).
  Location: index.html:846-849 (bell near-miss condition), 852-858 (nearMiss),
            712-718 (ringBell sets alarmActive=false but leaves alarm>0),
            836-845 (alarm fill is correctly gated by !room.broken)
  Explanation: the near-miss branch requires only
  `room.alarm > room.alarmMax*0.6` and `room.nearMissCd<=0`; it is NOT gated by
  `!room.broken` or `room.alarmActive`. ringBell() stops the meter
  (alarmActive=false) but does not zero `room.alarm`, and the player ends the
  ring standing on the bell (bd=0<1.0). So once the alarm passed 60% before the
  ring, the branch fires, sets nearMissCd=3, and fires again every 3s forever.
  Independent runtime verification (800x600): entered bell-chamber with
  alarm=48/alarmMax=60, moved onto the bell (room.broken=true, pos 3,2), then
  sampled score every 1.5s: 626 -> 676 -> 676 -> 726 -> 726 — increasing.
  Impact: corrupts the score / persisted best score (a scoring exploit, not a
  crash). Reachable in normal play by letting the alarm build past 60% before
  ringing.
  Fix approach: gate the bell near-miss branch with `!room.broken` (and ideally
  `room.alarmActive`), or zero `room.alarm` in ringBell() after the meter stops.
  Re-verify: after ringBell the score must stop increasing while idle on the bell.
