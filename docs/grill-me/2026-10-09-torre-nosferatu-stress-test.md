## Revision (post-grill) — Control tier raised to Tier 2

The user rejected the minimal-control framing: the grill optimized validity and
deadline, not creative involvement. Corrected conclusion:

- **Tier 2 — Direct the pipeline.** The root document becomes a **creative-direction
  brief**, not a vision-only note. Its contents are fed as **prompts** into
  `gdd_agent`, `world_agent`, `visual_agent`, and `audio_agent`, and used to steer
  each review/bugfix/juice loop. Every artifact is still generated by the agents.
- **Boundary unchanged:** no hand-writing `index.html`; no bypassing the approval gate.
- **New medium risk (added):** the brief must remain *direction* (intent + constraints),
  never *design authority* — if the GDD becomes a transcription of the brief, the
  "generated, not hand-built" claim weakens. Guard: the GDD is the agent's output; the
  brief is input the human paraphrases into prompts.
- **Scope note:** "brief ≤1 page" from the Low list is superseded — Tier 2 briefs are
  larger by design. Bound instead by *function* (direction), not length.
