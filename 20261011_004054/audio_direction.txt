# AUDIO DIRECTION FOR: Torre Nosferatu

**Run:** 20261010_095651 · **Source:** `gdd.md` (§6 Audio Direction, §2.7 Dread, §2.8 snuff/guttering/death, §4.2 wax) constrained by `direction.txt` §12.
**Hard constraints (from brief §12, treated as non-negotiable):**
1. Rule zero — silence is a tool; ambient bed sits at the edge of hearing; a cut to silence is an event.
2. The clock you hear — one fire/tick per wax burn, imperceptibly faster as wax drops; **killing the tick is the zero-wax cue**.
3. One pervasive low-frequency drone from turn 1, **rising with Dread tier**.
4. Direction/distance — creature cues panned + attenuated, quieter outside the radius; **Orlok's footsteps are the one thing that grows louder as light fails**.
5. Escalation arc — *snuff* = sharp cutoff + lone match-strike; *guttering dark* = drone swells + heartbeat + Orlok theme resolves; *death* = drone stops dead.
6. Five survivors, distinct minimal voices (hum / wheeze / whisper), speech low-passed and close.
7. Web Audio, **procedural only** (oscillators + noise, no sample assets).
8. Music/SFX toggles wired in **both Pause and Settings**.
9. Non-diegetic music sparse, reserved for terminal beats; duck ambience under Orlok's theme.

## 0. REVISION (2026-10-10) — audibility fix

The original mix targeted the ambient bed at **−34…−38 dB ("edge of hearing")**
and used a **38 Hz-only** drone. Implemented faithfully, that made the bed
inaudible on laptop/phone speakers (38 Hz is below their reproduction floor) —
the only audible element was the broadband UI click, perceived as *static*.
Superseded targets, now implemented (`index.html` `MIX` table):

- **Bed on the MUSIC bus** (drone + fire + wind), so the Music toggle is live.
  Cues (tick, wax, match, creature, voices, UI) stay on the SFX bus.
- **Drone re-voiced**: 38 Hz sub + detune (38·1.01) **plus harmonics 76/114/152/228/304 Hz**
  through a lowpass opened to ~450 Hz; level raised to ~**−18 dB** channel gain.
- **Fire ~−24 dB**, **wind ~−26 dB** (from −36/−38 dB).
- **Dread-tier melodic layer** (220/233 Hz) on the Music bus, rising with tier.
- **Orlok's theme** (organ sine + detuned saw + heartbeat) on the Music bus at guttering dark.
- UI click softened from a 3 kHz noise burst to a short quiet tonal tick.

Any dB figure below predating this block is **historical**; the `MIX` table is
authoritative. Verification: `window.audioTest(scenario,seed,opts)` renders
offline and asserts per-scenario RMS (bed rms≥0.02 & bandRms>300Hz≥0.003; cues
peak≥0.01; muted rms≤0.001).

## 0b. REVISION (2026-10-11) — organ bed replaces the drone (user-directed)

**This supersedes the §12 "one pervasive low-frequency drone" rule and the
rule-zero "silence is the default, music withheld" stance for this entry.**
The user judged the implemented bed (sub-bass + harmonics + broad-band
fire/wind noise) as *static*, and directed that it be replaced with actual
music appropriate to the setting. The superseding design:

- **Ambient music floor = a phrase-based pipe organ.** An **original** 4-note
  minor lament cell (D minor / Aeolian) — deliberately **not** a Dies Irae
  quote — played on an additive drawbar organ (sines at 0.5/1/1.5/2/3/4/6/8×,
  two slightly-detuned voices for chorus) through a **procedural cathedral
  reverb** (a send; IR = exponentially-decaying filtered noise). One phrase,
  then a long silence (`CYCLE_MIN..CYCLE_MAX` = 9–14 s), so silence remains an
  event — §12 rule zero survives in *density*, not in kind.
- **Choir layer.** Three detuned sawtooths through parallel formant bandpasses
  (≈320/800/2300 Hz) with a **capped** breathy-noise component (≤25% of the
  tonal level — chosen specifically so the layer cannot read as hiss).
  `setDread` fades it in from tier 2; the organ registration also opens with
  tier.
- **Candle crackle replaces the fire/wind hiss.** Sparse, irregular
  band-passed noise *bursts* (not a continuous noise bed) — the broadband hiss
  was a principal source of the "static" reading.
- **Bus routing (user-directed).** Organ + choir + reverb on the **MUSIC** bus;
  crackle and all event cues on the **SFX** bus. This departs from §6's "drone
  is ambient/SFX" guidance; Music-off now silences the bed and Orlok's theme
  (footsteps survive on SFX).
- **Offline contract.** `window.audioTest('bed',…)` forces the opening organ
  phrase at t=0 (the bed is event-based), so the 3 s render always contains
  music and the `bed` floors (rms≥0.02, bandRms>300Hz≥0.003) stay meaningful.
  Measured across seeds 1–20261010: rms 0.0245–0.0297, bandRms 0.0167–0.0194.
- **Superseded:** the `drone`/`fire`/`wind` mix entries and the 38 Hz/sub
  discussion above are historical; the `MIX` table
  (`organ`/`choir`/`crackle`/`reverbWet`) is authoritative.

## 1. THREE AUDIO DIRECTION CONCEPTS

### Concept A — "The Room Breathes" (diegetic-first, minimal score)
- **Style:** Pure soundscape. No musical themes except Orlok's. Every audible element is a physical event in the tower (fire, stone, wind, creatures, survivors). The drone is the building itself.
- **Instrumentation:** Noise-buffer fire crackle (band-passed), filtered noise "wind through stairs," contact-mic-style stone creaks, sub-bass drone (two detuned sines + low noise through lowpass), procedural creature textures.
- **Mood:** Cold, patient, claustrophobic. The tower is an organism; the player is an intrusion.
- **Fit:** Perfect match to "rule zero" and the candle-as-clock conceit. Risky: with no melodic anchor, the guttering-dark payload (Orlok's theme "resolving") has to earn its entrance from silence — a big contrast swing, but that is exactly the brief's stated design.

### Concept B — "Percussive Organ, Distant Howls" (gothic-scored, music-forward)
- **Style:** A sparse, slow gothic-horror score built on a held organ pedal and struck low bells, layered over the soundscape. Music present from the start, rising with Dread.
- **Instrumentation:** Sine/FM organ pedal, detuned saw pad through lowpass, procedural bell partials (additive sine stacks), timpani-like filtered noise hits, the drone as harmonic bed.
- **Mood:** 1922 *Nosferatu* / Hammer grandeur — theatrical dread, deliberately "scored."
- **Fit:** Directly echoes `direction.txt` §2 tone ("percussive organ, distant howls"). Risky against rule zero: continuous music erodes the silence-as-tool contrast and fights the tick and creature cues for attention. Needs aggressive ducking to work.

### Concept C — "Weather and the Tick" (hybrid: soundscape + one reserved theme)
- **Style:** Concept A's diegetic soundscape as the permanent ambient floor, plus exactly **one** recurring musical motif — Orlok's theme — withheld entirely until the guttering dark. The Dread drone is the middle layer.
- **Instrumentation:** Concept A's fire/wind/stone; the Dread drone (sub-bass detuned sines + lowpassed noise); Orlok's theme as a single slow minor-second-heavy cell (low organ sine + detuned saw, one heartbeat kick).
- **Mood:** The tower is weather; Orlok is the only music. Silence is the default; dread is a rising floor; the theme is a knife saved for the end.
- **Fit:** Satisfies rule zero (silence is default), the "one pervasive drone," the "sparse non-diegetic music reserved for terminal beats," and gives the escalation arc its three distinct tiers. The organ/howls of Concept B survive as *texture*, not as wall-to-wall score.

## 2. SELECTION PROCESS (transparent)

**Scoring 1–5 per criterion; weights from the brief's own emphasis.**

| Criterion (weight) | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule zero / silence-as-tool honored | 5 | 2 | 5 |
| Candle-as-clock "tick" legibility | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| Drone rises with Dread, pervasive from turn 1 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| Snuff vs. guttering vs. death *audibly distinct* | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Procedural-only, single-file lightness | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Sparse music, reserved for terminal beats | 4 | 1 | 5 |
| Five survivor voices come through | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Risk of masking directional creature cues | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| **Weighted total** | **34** | **23** | **37** |

- **A** is the purest expression of the brief, but its refusal of *any* musical anchor makes the "guttering dark = Orlok's theme resolves" beat depend on a contrast the brief explicitly wants — yet also leaves the middle Dread tiers with no melodic escalation, only drone amplitude. Strong, but one dimension thin.
- **B** best honors §2's "percussive organ, distant howls" literally, but a continuous score violates rule zero and the "music stays non-diegetic, sparse, reserved for terminal beats" constraint. Music-forward is the wrong reading of *this* brief.
- **C** keeps A's silence discipline, keeps B's gothic vocabulary as texture, and gives the escalation arc a place to go (ambient → drone → theme). It is the only concept that satisfies every §12 bullet without tension.

**Decision: Concept C — "Weather and the Tick."** Elements of B (organ timbre, distant howls as environmental texture) are retained as instrumentation, not as score. Elements of A are retained wholesale as the ambient bed.

## 3. CONCRETE SOUND LIST (name · trigger · Web Audio synthesis · mix notes)

All nodes created once at unlock into a persistent graph; per-event sounds are short-lived oscillators/noise bursts. **No samples, no fetch, no base64 audio.**

### 3.1 Persistent layers (always running, gain-modulated)

| Name | Trigger | Web Audio synthesis | Mix notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **drone-bed** | Scheduler start (first turn); runs the whole run | Two `OscillatorNode`s (sine) at e.g. 38 Hz and 38·1.01 Hz (detune ~8¢) → `GainNode` → `BiquadFilterNode` lowpass (~120 Hz) → master. Plus a `noise buffer` → lowpass (~90 Hz) for sub-body. | Floor of the mix, **audible at Dread 0** (~−18 dB; re-voiced per §0 — sub + harmonics to ~304 Hz). |
| **drone-dread-lift** | Dread tier changes (Calm→…→Hunted-full) | Modulate a dedicated `GainNode` on the drone ladder: per tier, +2–3 dB, plus a slight lowpass opening (~120→260 Hz) to add "weight". Optional: slowly ramp the detune from 8¢→20¢ as tiers rise (beating = unease). | The *only* continuous element allowed to rise with Dread. Capped below Orlok's theme and never above −20 dB. |
| **ambient-fire** | Always during GAMEPLAY | Looping noise buffer through a bandpass (~1500 Hz, Q~0.7) with a slow random LFO on gain (flame flicker). A second, quieter noise layer lowpassed (~400 Hz) for wax-melt body. | Audible ambient floor (~−24 dB; re-voiced per §0). Guttering dark re-shapes it (§3.3); snuff cuts it dead (§3.2). |
| **ambient-tower** | Always during GAMEPLAY | Noise → bandpass swept slowly (600–900 Hz) + highpass "wind through stairs"; very slow random gain modulation. | ~−26 dB (re-voiced per §0). Provides the "not silence" floor so a real cut reads. |

### 3.2 Event sounds (short-lived)

| Name | Trigger | Web Audio synthesis | Mix notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **wax-tick** | One per wax burn (every turn / costly action) | Short noise burst (~25 ms) → bandpass ~2200 Hz, sharp attack/decay; plus a tiny low sine "thump" (~110 Hz, 40 ms) for body. | The clock. Present but soft (~−26 dB). |
| **wax-tick-tempo** | Continuous while GAMEPLAY | A tempo value drives tick scheduling: `interval = base − k·(startWax − wax)` (e.g. base 900 ms → ~600 ms at 0 wax), **imperceptibly** per-turn. | Player *feels* acceleration before reading the meter. **At wax = 0 the scheduler stops; killing the tick is the zero-wax cue** (no tick = the silence that means death is coming). |
| **wax-found** | `wax-cake` picked up | Rising two-note sine arpeggio (e.g. 392→523 Hz, short) + soft noise shimmer; brief duck of ambient-fire so the positive beat reads. | The <30 s "WAX FOUND" success cue (GDD §4.4). Brighter than anything else; the only "warm" reward sound. |
| **match-strike** | Match used to re-light (snuff recovery, light-trap, guttering recovery) | Noise burst → bandpass sweep (800→3000 Hz) with fast decay = the strike; then a short noise "whoomph" (lowpass ~500 Hz) as flame catches; ambient-fire gain ramps back over ~400 ms. | **Lone, dry, close** — this is the sound of hope. Plays both after a snuff and during guttering recovery. |
| **match-pickup** | `matches` thing collected | Small metallic `triangle` ping (~1200 Hz, 80 ms) + noise click. | Distinct from wax-found; quieter, drier. |
| **key-found** | `exit-key` acquired | Low bell partials (additive sines 220/330/440 Hz, long-ish decay) + a single deep sine swell. | Rarer and heavier than wax-found; signals "you can leave now." |
| **lore-found** | `lore-fragment` / lore room entered | Soft airy noise pad (bandpass sweep up, ~1200→2600 Hz, ~600 ms) + faint high sine. | Non-survival, so *not* celebratory — curious, cold, whispered. Reading in the journal is free and silent. |
| **candle-radius-change** | Effective radius step (wax thresholds 24/18/12/6/0) | Filter sweep on ambient-fire (brighter/higher as radius grows, duller as it shrinks) + a soft rising/falling sine glide. | Sub-threshold — the player should notice the room got darker *sound and*, not a UI beep. |
| **dread-tier-shift** | Dread crosses a tier boundary | A single low "weather" swell: filtered noise gust + drone-ladder step (the drone-dread-lift ramp) + a very soft detuned low sine pair beating into phase. | Never a stinger. Dread is weather, not a deed-ledger (GDD §2.7) — no "bad choice" chime. |
| **creature-cue-{bats,rats,spiders,wolves,shades,light-eater}** | A creature enters/moves within the active floor, on its turn | Per creature: bats = clusters of short high FM squeaks (sine carrier 3–5 kHz, fast downward sweeps); rats = filtered noise scurry bursts (bandpass ~2.5 kHz, rapid amplitude chops); spiders = dry tick/rattle (short noise clicks + high bandpass); wolves = a long descending detuned saw/sine howl (~300→180 Hz, slow vibrato); shades = breathy whisper noise pad (bandpass ~900 Hz, slow LFO); light-eater = a sucking lowpass-inverted noise swell that *eats* the ambient (momentary duck + upward filter pull). | **Panned by direction and attenuated by distance to the player. Quiter outside the radius.** `StereoPannerNode` + `GainNode` scaled by tile distance; beyond radius gain floor ~30% of in-radius. |
| **survivor-voice-{vlask,marta,ilona,petr,nadja}** | Survivor encountered / speaks on co-location or social action | One `OscillatorNode`/noise recipe per NPC, run through a **lowpass (~500–900 Hz) "close speech" filter** to read as muffled near-speech/voice, not dialogue: **Vlask** = low clipped square-ish buzz (hoarding, counting); **Sister Marta** = measured thin sine with slow amplitude phrasing; **Ilona** = the same sine *with a wheeze* (noise layer gated under every phrase); **Petr** = a young, uneven hum (gentle vibrato, slightly sharp); **Nadja** = bright quick triangle chirps (transactional). | Speech **low-passed and close** (per brief). Pan center; slight proximity gain when co-located. Disposition changes are audible via a pitch/timbre offset (warmer = softer lowpass; hostile = harder, brighter edge) so stance reads without words. |
| **social-action-{warm,cold}** | Any of the six social actions resolves | Warm: a soft two-note rising interval (the candle sharing), gentle. Cold: a low, gritty noise-swipe + a momentary drone flinch. | Warm acts give Dread −1 (drone lifts down momentarily); cold acts raise Dread (drone steps up). The sound *is* the mechanical consequence. |
| **zone-bonus** | First entry to a new floor | Deep bell + airy chime, brief; then ambient shifts (new floor = fresh ambient re-seed). | Marks the descent as progress (GDD §4.4 +100/floor). |
| **near-miss** | Grazing a creature / escaping at wax ≤ 3 | Sharp filtered noise "whoosh" + a single high sine stab, very short. | Adrenaline punctuation, not a melody. |
| **snuff** | Candle snuffed by creature/`light-trap`/`light-eater` | **Sharp cutoff:** ambient-fire and drone both cut to near-zero in ~30 ms; a hard low thud; then a moment of near-total silence; then a **lone match-strike** if/when the player spends a match. | **Visually/audibly distinct from guttering.** Hard edge. Recoverable (GDD §2.8). |
| **guttering-enter** | Wax reaches 0 | (See arc §4) Drone swells; heartbeat enters; ambient-fire gutters (slow wobble, not cutoff); the wax-tick scheduler **stops**. | The silence of the tick + the swell of the drone = "you are past the clock." |
| **orlok-theme** | GUTTERING_DARK active | Orlok's single motif: a low organ sine (~65 Hz) + detuned saw through lowpass, playing one slow minor-second-heavy cell; a procedural "heartbeat" (low sine thump pair, ~1 Hz, rising tempo) underneath; footsteps (§3.3). Ambience ducks under it. | The **only** music in the game; withheld until the terminal beat (brief: "reserve it for the terminal beats"). |
| **orlok-footstep** | GUTTERING_DARK, per grace turn / Orlok closing | Low thump (sine ~55 Hz, 120 ms) + gravel noise burst; **gain increases each grace turn** and with lower remaining wax/grace. | **The one thing that grows louder as light fails.** Panned toward Orlok's approach. |
| **death** | Grace spent + Orlok arrives (GAME_OVER) | **Drone stops dead:** all persistent nodes hard-cut to silence in ~20 ms; a final low grave thud; then true silence under the game-over text. | The harshest cut in the game. Silence *is* "Count Orlok takes you." |
| **victory-full** | Reach exit with key | The drone resolves upward and fades; a warm major-ish sine cadence (two or three slow notes) blooms; ambient opens. | Relief = the drone finally *leaves*. |
| **victory-borrowed** | Slip out through a survivor's door | Same warm resolve but thinner/short + a soft question-mark interval; ambience returns a touch colder. | Audibly the "lesser ending" (GDD §2.9 / brief §8). |
| **ui-click / ui-toggle** | Any button, toggle, screen nav | Very short filtered noise tick + tiny sine blip. Toggle on/off = two-tone up/down. | UI stays dry and quiet so it never pollutes the soundscape. |

### 3.3 The "clock you hear" — exact wiring
- One `wax-tick` per wax burn, driven by the turn handler.
- Tempo: `interval = 900 − 300·(waxStart − wax)/waxStart` ms (design intent; implementer may tune within the "imperceptibly faster" constraint).
- **On wax = 0 the tick scheduler is stopped and never restarted for the run.** The absence of the tick is the zero-wax cue. Do not replace it with a different continuous sound — *kill* it.
- The tick must remain audible but subordinate to drone and creature cues (≈−26 dB) so it never masks a direction cue.

## 4. ESCALATION ARC (three terminal states, audibly distinct)

```
CALM (Dread 0)        ambient fire + tower wind at the edge of hearing
   │                  tick slow; drone barely there (−34 dB)
   ▼  Dread rises
TENSION (T1–T3)       drone ladder steps up each tier (+2–3 dB, filter opens);
   │                  tick accelerates with wax; creature cues denser/panned
   ▼  wax → 0        ── tick scheduler STOPS ──
GUTTERING DARK        drone SWELLS; heartbeat enters; ambient-fire gutters
   │                  (slow wobble); Orlok theme RESOLVES; footsteps grow
   │                  louder each grace turn; ambience ducks under the theme
   ├── match found ──► match-strike → ambient-fire ramps back → tick RESUMES
   │                   (recovered; back to TENSION)
   ├── exit reached ─► VICTORY (full/borrowed resolve)
   ▼  grace spent
DEATH                 drone STOPS DEAD (hard ~20 ms cut) → grave thud →
                      true silence under "Count Orlok takes you"

SNUFF (separate from the arc)  sharp cutoff of fire+drone → hard thud →
       near-silence → lone match-strike → fire returns. Distinct from the
       GUTTERING swell. Recoverable unless wax is already 0.
```

**Distinction contract (must not blur):**
- **Snuff** = *instant hard edge* → match.
- **Guttering** = *slow swell*, heartbeat, theme, growing footsteps.
- **Death** = *the swell cut dead* → silence.

## 5. DIRECTION & DISTANCE MODEL

- Every creature cue and Orlok's footsteps are routed through `StereoPannerNode` (pan ∝ horizontal offset from the player) and a `GainNode` (gain ∝ distance attenuation).
- **Outside-radius rule:** a cue whose source tile is beyond the candle radius plays at a reduced floor gain (~30% of in-radius), still panned. The player hears the tower's neighbourhoods faintly and the immediate circle clearly.
- **Exception:** Orlok's footsteps *invert* the distance curve during GUTTERING_DARK — as wax/grace falls, gain rises regardless of radius.
- Survivor voices are center-panned and close (lowpassed), because co-location is the trigger.

## 6. TOGGLE WIRING (Music / SFX) — required in Pause AND Settings

- One shared audio state object, e.g. `audioState = { music: true, sfx: true, unlocked: false }`, read by the `AudioEngine`.
- **Music toggle** gates the *musical* layers only: `orlok-theme` (and any terminal resolve cue). Because the drone is the "tower's mood" and pervasive by rule, treat the **drone as ambient/SFX**, not music — so the music toggle is effectively "Orlok's theme on/off."
- **SFX toggle** gates everything else: ambient bed, drone, ticks, all event sounds, voices. (If a single "SFX" toggle must also kill the drone for player comfort, implement a master SFX gain that mutes the whole non-music bus; the drone is on that bus.)
- Toggles must be **two-way bound**: the Pause control and the Settings control reflect the same state; flipping one updates the other on next render/open.
- **Separate master gains:** `musicGain` and `sfxGain` nodes in the graph; toggles set their gains to 0 / restore value (ramp over ~50 ms to avoid clicks, never hard-disconnect — a hard cut should be reserved for the *diegetic* snuff/death beats, not the settings UI).
- Toggles act **immediately** and persist for the session. No external storage required.
- Audio must not start until a **user gesture unlock** (GDD §10.5): the Title "Start"/first tap calls `ctx.resume()`; all scheduled nodes are created lazily after unlock.

## 7. TECHNICAL APPROACH (single-file, Web Audio, procedural)

- **One `AudioContext`**, created/resumed on first user gesture (`touchstart`/`mousedown`/keydown). No `AudioBufferSource` sample assets — all sources are `OscillatorNode` and procedurally-generated `AudioBuffer` noise (white/pink built with `Math.random`).
- **Persistent graph** built at unlock: `masterGain → { musicGain, sfxGain }`; under `sfxGain`: `drone-bus`, `ambient-bus`, `event-bus`; under `musicGain`: `orlok-bus`. Creature/voice/step cues each get a per-source `StereoPannerNode` + `GainNode`.
- **Event sounds** are fire-and-forget: create oscillator/noise → envelope (`GainNode` with `setValueAtTime`/`linearRampToValueAtTime`) → auto-disconnect on `onended`. Never accumulate nodes.
- **Turn-driven, not frame-driven:** tick tempo, drone tier, footsteps, and heartbeat are updated on discrete game events (turn resolved, Dread changed, wax changed, grace turn) — matching the turn-based loop (GDD §10.5). A single `setInterval`/scheduler drives the wax tick; all other timing is event-driven.
- **Determinism-friendly:** sound *choice* may vary with `window.SEED` for texture variety, but the tick tempo, tier mapping, and arc transitions are driven by `window.systemState()` (Dread) and wax — so behavior is reproducible under `test_seed`.
- **Safety:** all audio calls wrapped so a missing/blocked `AudioContext` degrades silently (game remains playable); toggles and unlock checks guard every entry point; no unbounded schedulers (the wax-tick interval is always cleared on state change/stop).
- **Budget:** zero network, zero base64; worst case a handful of oscillators + a few KB of generated noise buffers — negligible against the single-file constraint.

## 8. MIX PRIORITIES (loudness ladder, quiet→loud)
1. **Silence** — reserved; the most important "instrument."
2. ambient-tower wind (~−38 dB)
3. ambient-fire (~−36 dB)
4. drone bed at Calm (~−34 dB) → rising to ~−20 dB at Hunted-full
5. ui (~−30 dB)
6. wax tick (~−26 dB)
7. creature cues (in-radius ~−22 dB; out-of-radius ~−32 dB)
8. survivor voices (~−22 dB, lowpassed/close)
9. Orlok theme + footsteps (swell to the loudest sustained element during GUTTERING_DARK; ambience ducks under it)
10. Snuff / death cuts — instantaneous departures from the ladder (the event is the *absence*).

**Ducking rule:** whenever `orlok-theme` is active, `ambient-bus` and `drone-bus` are ducked (ramp down ~4–6 dB) so the theme and footsteps dominate the terminal beat.

---

*End of audio direction — Torre Nosferatu ("Weather and the Tick"). Procedural Web Audio, single file, toggles in Pause + Settings.*
