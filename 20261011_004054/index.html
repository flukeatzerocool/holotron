How to Play

The candle is your clock, your health, and your map. Every turn burns 1 wax. As wax falls your light radius closes in (but never below 2 tiles while lit). At 0 wax the guttering dark begins.

Move: tap/click an adjacent room tile, or use WASD / arrow keys. One move = one wax. Space waits (burns 1 wax).

One key per floor. Find the floor's key, then reach a Descending Stair and press Descend. The stair gate stays locked until you hold that floor's key. On the ground floor the key also opens the exit.

Creatures: Run costs wax (candle stays lit); Endure snuffs the candle and costs no wax — spend a match to re-light it. No match, no light. There is no HP and no battle.

Matches re-light a snuffed candle (they do not add wax).

Survivors (floor 2 down): six actions. Warm acts (share light, ally, bargain, trade) lower Dread but cost light. Cold acts (steal, threaten) buy light now but raise Dread.

Dread (0–8) is weather: it starts strangers warier and speeds Orlok when your flame dies. Warm acts make it roll away.

Descend: stand in a Descending Stair holding the floor's key and press Descend (or move down). Reach the ground floor with its key to escape.

Lose: the only death is Count Orlok after the grace turns run out while your candle is out.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move · Space wait/confirm · Esc pause · 1–6 social actions.
