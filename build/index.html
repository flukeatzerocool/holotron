HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: every room posts a rule. The rule is a LIE. Break it to open the doors.
  • Move: tap the tile you want to step onto (or use the D-pad).
  • Doors: after you break the rule, tap a glowing cyan door to travel.
  • Special: tap FLIP (or swipe up) to flip gravity; tap a wall to eat it; move onto the bell to ring it; tap the master rulebook to tear its page.
  • Score: moves +1, room entry +50, rule shatter +100×room, things +25, near-miss +50.

“The rule is ALWAYS lying — that’s the whole game.”
